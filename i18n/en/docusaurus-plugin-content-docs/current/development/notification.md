# Notification

The Weiyu notification module gives organizations a unified place to deliver important internal messages. System reminders, team announcements, and platform-level notices can all be managed within one notification center. Compared with temporary messages buried in chat groups, email threads, or verbal communication, this module is better suited for formal, trackable, and structured internal communication.

For non-technical teams, it can be understood as an internal announcement center built directly into the Weiyu admin console. It is suitable for daily operations, policy communication, urgent reminders, maintenance announcements, and security notices.

## Typical Use Cases

- Publish company-wide announcements such as holiday schedules, policy updates, or attendance changes.
- Send targeted reminders to a specific department, such as customer service shift changes or sales updates.
- Deliver a direct notice to an individual employee, such as an approval reminder or handoff instruction.
- Announce maintenance windows, service adjustments, or upgrade notices.
- Issue security-related alerts such as account risk notices or password update reminders.

## Why This Module Matters

### 1. A Unified Notification Entry Point

Weiyu centralizes notifications in the admin system so employees can review messages from a dedicated personal notification page. The notification bell in the top-right corner also shows unread counts and a preview of recent unread items. This helps reduce the risk of important information being overlooked.

### 2. Hierarchical Delivery for Better Organization Management

Notifications are not limited to mass broadcasting. The current version supports multiple delivery scopes:

- Platform-level notifications: suitable for platform-wide announcements.
- Organization-level notifications: suitable for sending messages to all members within an organization.
- Department-level notifications: suitable for communicating with a specific department.
- User-level notifications: suitable for precise one-to-one reminders.

This allows organizations to control who receives what, improving efficiency while reducing unnecessary noise.

### 3. Clear Read and Unread Status

Notifications support unread and read states. Employees can open a message, review the details, and mark it as read. They can also mark all notifications as read in one action. For managers, this creates a more formal and manageable process than sending messages only through chat.

### 4. Clear Notification Types

The module currently supports several notification categories:

- General: for day-to-day operational communication.
- Announcement: for formal announcements and official notices.
- Maintenance: for upgrades, maintenance windows, or service adjustments.
- Security: for account safety, permission changes, and risk alerts.

These categories help employees quickly understand the nature of each message.

## How It Is Used in Weiyu

The notification module currently provides three main entry points in the admin console:

- Personal notifications: employees view messages sent specifically to them under Settings - Notification.
- Team notifications: organization administrators manage and send organization-level notifications under Team - Notification.
- Platform notifications: super administrators publish platform-level notices under Super - Notification.

At the same time, the notification bell in the upper-right corner shows unread counts and previews several recent unread messages, making it easy to jump directly into notification details.

## Benefits for Managers

For business owners, operations teams, HR, administrative staff, or service managers, the value of this module is straightforward:

- It provides a more formal channel than chat for announcements and policy communication.
- It supports precise targeting by organization, department, or individual user.
- It keeps every notification structured with title, body, type, sender, and timestamp.
- It helps teams manage notification records in a centralized way.

## Benefits for Employees

For frontline employees, service agents, sales teams, or operators, the experience is simple and familiar:

- Unread notifications are visible immediately after opening the admin console.
- The bell icon provides a quick preview of recent unread items.
- The full content can be reviewed from the notification page.
- Once opened, messages can move into a read state for easier personal follow-up.

## A Typical Workflow

For example, if a company wants to announce a customer service shift change:

1. An administrator opens Team - Notification.
2. The notification type is selected, such as General or Announcement.
3. The delivery scope is selected, such as Department.
4. The target department, title, and body are filled in.
5. The message is sent to the selected recipients.
6. Employees see the unread reminder in their own notification center.
7. After opening the message, the notification is updated as read.

The full process stays inside the platform and supports a more standardized internal communication workflow.

## How To Position It Externally

When introducing this feature to customers, the Weiyu notification module can be described as:

> A built-in internal notification center for enterprise collaboration, supporting hierarchical delivery, unread reminders, notification detail views, and centralized message management for internal announcements and important reminders.

It is especially suitable for organizations that:

- Have multiple collaborative roles such as customer service, sales, and operations.
- Need to publish frequent internal announcements, policy updates, or operational reminders.
- Want to consolidate internal notices into one unified platform instead of relying on chat groups or verbal communication.

## Current Scope and Capability Boundaries

To keep external messaging accurate, this module is best positioned as an in-system notification center or back-office notification center. It already supports complete backend viewing, sending, unread reminders, and hierarchical delivery.

Current capabilities that can be promoted with confidence include:

- In-app notification delivery
- Unread count reminders
- Recent unread message previews
- Delivery by platform, organization, department, or individual user
- Read and unread status management
- Notification type categorization

Unless additional integrations have already been delivered, it is better not to describe this module as a full omnichannel notification platform covering SMS, email, or mobile push.

## Summary

The core value of the Weiyu notification module is not simply sending another message. Its real value is helping organizations make internal communication more unified, more formal, and easier to manage. For enterprises that want better control over message scope, stronger information delivery, and fewer missed notices, this is a practical foundational module.
