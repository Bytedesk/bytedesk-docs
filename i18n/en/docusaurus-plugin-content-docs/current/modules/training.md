# Weiyu Customer Service & Employee Training

## Overview

Training Management provides customer service centers and service teams with an integrated framework of "learning resources + online assessments + AI role-play coaching." It helps enterprises convert scattered business knowledge, scripts, and service procedures into structured assets that can be learned, tested, and reviewed.

After onboarding study and tests, new staff can practice real-world scenarios through AI role-play and corrective feedback before handling customers independently. Existing staff can also improve consistency and professionalism through periodic assessments and targeted coaching.

## Objectives

- **Shorten onboarding time**: Upgrade from passive reading to a closed loop of learn-test-practice-go-live.
- **Standardize service quality**: Use unified scripts, workflows, and knowledge content to reduce answer variance.
- **Make performance measurable**: Track learning progress, test scores, and coaching outcomes for management decisions.
- **Enable continuous improvement**: Feed back wrong answers and high-frequency issues to optimize content and strategy.

## Core Capabilities

### 1) Learning resource integration (courses and knowledge)

- **Unified learning portal**: Centralize business knowledge, process guides, and service standards for easy access.
- **Role/stage-based organization**: Build learning paths by role, business line, and training stage.

### 2) Online exams and question-bank evaluation

- **Online assessments**: Run onboarding exams, periodic evaluations, and topic-based validation.
- **Question-bank management**: Maintain categorized question banks (product knowledge, process standards, compliance points, FAQs).
- **Passing thresholds and retakes**: Configure pass lines and retake rules to control training quality.

### 3) AI role-play coaching (scenario simulation)

- **Realistic scenario simulation**: Train with typical service scenarios (consultation, complaints, escalation, exception handling).
- **Progressive skill building**: Move from basic Q&A to complex context handling step by step.
- **Instant feedback and correction**: Provide focused feedback on verification, process completeness, script quality, and risk prompts.

### 4) Closed-loop training-to-readiness management

- **Learn-test-practice-go-live**: Define readiness criteria and only enable independent handling after completion.
- **Targeted upskilling**: Launch focused retraining and re-evaluation on weak topics and process nodes.

### 5) Training dashboards and reports (examples)

- **Learning progress**: Course completion rate, learning duration, path completion.
- **Exam performance**: Pass rate, average score, difficulty distribution, weak knowledge points.
- **Practice performance**: Practice count, completion rate, common error types, improvement trends.

### 6) Permissions and compliance

- Role-based access control for admin, training manager, team lead, and learner.
- Audit trails for training records, exam records, and key configuration changes.

## Typical Workflow

1. Organize and import business knowledge and courses; build directories and learning paths
2. Configure question banks and exam plans; set pass thresholds and retake rules
3. New employees follow paths and complete online exams
4. Qualified employees enter AI role-play practice for assigned scenarios
5. Grant independent service permissions after readiness criteria are met
6. Run periodic retraining and reassessment for in-service staff

## Common Use Cases

- **New-hire bootcamp**: Build standardized training flow and reduce dependence on informal mentoring.
- **New product/policy rollout**: Quickly train frontline staff and keep answer quality consistent.
- **Service quality initiatives**: Target complaint hotspots and frequent errors with dedicated drills.
- **Team capability alignment**: Use dashboards to identify gaps and align performance across teams.

## AI Role-play Scenario Library (examples)

To make training practical and realistic, AI coaching supports scenario libraries by industry and business line:

- **Basic consultation**: Fee/balance/progress inquiries, branch/time info, policy explanation.
	- Focus: verification completeness, answer consistency, next-step guidance.
- **Service handling**: Document preparation, process guidance, online form/operation support.
	- Focus: end-to-end process closure, risk prompts, reduced repeated effort for customers.
- **Exception handling**: Device/system failures, unavailable service paths, weak-network interruptions.
	- Focus: diagnosis clarity, troubleshooting steps, escalation timing.
- **Complaint de-escalation**: Emotional customers, repeated urging, disputed communication, service recovery.
	- Focus: empathy expression, promise boundaries, solution and timeline clarity.
- **Compliance and risk control**: identity verification, privacy protection, sensitive data handling, key-term disclosure.
	- Focus: process compliance, avoiding over-commitment, proper traceability guidance.

## Coaching Evaluation Dimensions (examples)

AI coaching provides measurable feedback across five dimensions:

- **Knowledge accuracy**: Correctness and alignment with standard responses.
- **Process completeness**: Coverage of required steps and critical questions.
- **Communication quality**: Clarity, professionalism, and standard phrasing.
- **Service experience**: Empathy, guidance quality, and customer effort reduction.
- **Compliance and risk**: Required warnings/verification and compliant expression.
