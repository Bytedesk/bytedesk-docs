# Data Dashboard

The data dashboard shows conversation records, customer profiles, and offline messages.

![Data dashboard](/img/manual/agent/data.png)

## Conversation Records

The default list of conversation records:

- Filter historical conversations by time range, status, agent, keyword, and more
- Click a conversation to view the full messages and both parties' information
- Export conversations for backup

## Customer Management

View and maintain customer profiles:

- **Profile**: nickname, contact details, source channel, tags, etc.
- **History**: the customer's past conversations
- **Tickets**: the customer's past tickets

## Message Management

Review offline messages left by visitors when agents were offline or outside service hours; follow up one by one, and mark them handled or convert them into tickets when done.

:::note Note
Visible data scope depends on permissions; advanced reports such as online-service and ticket statistics depend on edition and permissions.
:::
