# Visitor Quick Start

The Bytedesk visitor chat widget brings online consultation to websites, apps, and business systems. You can start a conversation as an anonymous visitor, or sign in to review your history and tickets. The buttons actually shown on the page are configured by the enterprise administrator and may vary slightly between entry points.

## Opening the Chat Window

The visitor chat is usually opened in one of the following ways:

- Click the online-chat icon in the lower-right corner of the website and start chatting in the floating window;
- Click a customer service link provided by the enterprise to open the conversation in a standalone page;
- Enter the embedded chat window from a business system, official account, or another page;
- Click a ticket entry in notifications or history to open "My Tickets".

After the page opens, the system automatically connects to the corresponding enterprise, agent workgroup, or bot based on the entry point. Do not modify the parameters in the address bar, otherwise the correct service entry may not be found.

## Getting to Know the Chat Interface

![Visitor chat interface](/img/manual/chat/gettting_started.png)

The chat page mainly consists of the following areas:

1. **Top bar**: shows the agent or bot name, with entries such as back and history.
2. **Message area**: shows the welcome message, quick questions, queue notices, and the conversation history.
3. **Input area**: type and send text; depending on configuration you can also send emojis, images, files, or voice.
4. **Extended toolbar**: may include entries for rating, leaving a message, products, orders, tickets, platform service, and audio/video calls.

## Completing Your First Consultation

1. Wait for the welcome message and quick questions to load.
2. Describe your problem in the input box. When possible, include the usage scenario, what happened, and the expected result.
3. Click the send button and wait for the bot or a human agent to reply.
4. If the bot does not solve the problem, click "Transfer to Agent"; when agents are busy, the page shows the queue status.
5. If agents are offline or outside service hours, follow the on-page prompt to leave a message with a way to contact you.
6. After the service ends, submit a star rating, resolution status, and feedback if a rating invitation appears.

## Using the Bot and Human Agents

The bot may first help you locate answers through the welcome message, popular questions, or "You may want to ask". Under the bot reply you may see "Helpful", "Not helpful", or "Transfer to Agent":

- If the problem is solved, click "Helpful";
- If the answer is inaccurate, click "Not helpful" and continue to add details;
- If a human is needed for account, order, or complex business issues, click "Transfer to Agent".

After transferring, pay attention to the queue notice. Repeatedly refreshing the page before entering a human conversation may affect your current queue position and conversation state.

## Submitting and Tracking Tickets

When a problem requires follow-up, open "Ticket" or "My Tickets" from the toolbar:

1. Create a ticket and fill in the title, problem description, urgency, and contact information;
2. Select the problem category, fill in extra fields, or upload attachments as required by the page;
3. After submission, check the status in the ticket list;
4. Open the details to view handling information and the processing flow;
5. When the ticket is resolved, confirm "Resolved" or "Unresolved" as prompted.

The ticket feature is configured by the enterprise. If no ticket button is shown at the current entry, ask a human agent to create one for you.

## Best Practices

- Do not send passwords, verification codes, bank card numbers, or other sensitive information in messages or attachments;
- Before uploading an image or file, make sure it contains no unrelated personal information;
- After a network interruption, wait for the page to recover automatically before checking whether the message was sent;
- For issues that need long-term follow-up, submit a ticket after signing in, so you can still view it after clearing browser data;
- If the page features or names differ from this document, the configuration currently enabled by the enterprise prevails.
