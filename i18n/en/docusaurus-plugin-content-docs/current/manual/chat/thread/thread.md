# Chat Conversation

The chat page is where visitors communicate with the bot or human agents, including sending messages, viewing history, and submitting ratings.

## Sending Messages

![Sending messages and bot replies](/img/manual/chat/thread/thread.png)

In the input area you can:

- Enter text and click the send button (or press Enter);
- Click the emoji button to insert an emoji;
- Click the image or file button to upload an attachment;
- Switch to voice mode to record and send a voice message.

The available options depend on enterprise configuration.

## Using the Toolbar

![Chat toolbar](/img/manual/chat/thread/toolbar.png)

The extended toolbar on the page may provide the following entries:

| Entry | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Rate | Submit a satisfaction rating after the service |
| Leave message | Leave a message when agents are offline |
| Product / Order | View product or order information |
| Ticket / My tickets | Create or view tickets |
| Platform service | Escalate to the platform service desk |
| Audio / Video call | Start a voice or video call with the agent |

## Transferring to a Human Agent

When the bot cannot solve your problem:

1. Click "Transfer to Agent" below the bot reply or in the toolbar;
2. If agents are busy, the page shows the queue status and estimated wait;
3. Once connected, describe your problem to the human agent.

Before the transfer completes, avoid refreshing the page repeatedly so the queue state is not lost.

## Leaving a Message

![Leave-message form](/img/manual/chat/thread/thread-2.png)

When agents are offline or outside service hours, you can submit a message:

1. Click "Leave message";
2. Fill in your name, contact information, and problem description;
3. Submit and wait for a reply from the enterprise.

## Service Rating

![Satisfaction rating](/img/manual/chat/thread/rate.png)

After the conversation ends, the page may invite you to rate the service:

1. Select a star level;
2. Optionally choose whether the problem was solved and leave comments;
3. Submit the rating.

## History and Conversation End

![History conversation list](/img/manual/chat/thread/history.png)

- Click the history entry at the top to view past conversations;
- After the conversation ends you can continue consulting through a new session;
- Signing in with the same account keeps history across devices.
