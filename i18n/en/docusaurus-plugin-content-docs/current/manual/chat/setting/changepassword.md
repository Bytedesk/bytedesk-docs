# Account and Password Security

For visitors who sign in with an account, keeping the account secure ensures history and tickets remain accessible.

## When to Change or Reset the Password

- Change the password promptly when you suspect it has been leaked or shared;
- Use the "Forgot password" entry on the login page to reset it via a bound phone number or email;
- If neither is bound, contact the enterprise administrator to reset it.

## Protecting Your Account

- Use a strong password with a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols, and avoid reusing passwords from other sites;
- Do not share the account or verification codes with anyone;
- Sign out promptly after use on public or shared devices;
- Bind a phone number and email to enable self-service recovery.

## Login Anomalies

| Symptom | Suggestion |
| --- | --- |
| Account or password forgotten | Reset via "Forgot password" or contact the administrator |
| Account locked after failed attempts | Wait for the lockout to end, or contact the administrator |
| Login succeeds but data is empty | Confirm you are using the same organization and server |
| Account logged in elsewhere | Change the password immediately and check device security |
