# My Tickets

"My Tickets" brings together ticket submission, status tracking, and result confirmation, suitable for problems that need follow-up handling.

## Opening the Ticket Center

![Visitor ticket center](/img/manual/ticket/gettting_started.png)

Open the ticket center from the chat toolbar or a ticket link provided by the enterprise. Depending on deployment, you may need to sign in first.

## Viewing and Searching Tickets

![Ticket list](/img/manual/ticket/list.png)

The ticket list shows your submitted tickets and their current status:

- Filter by status: pending, processing, resolved, closed, etc.;
- Search by ticket number, title, or keywords;
- Open a ticket to view the latest progress.

## Creating a Ticket

![Create-ticket form](/img/manual/ticket/create-filled.png)

1. Click "Create ticket";
2. Fill in the title, problem description, and urgency;
3. Select the problem category and fill in contact information as required;
4. Upload attachments such as screenshots if needed;
5. Submit the ticket.

After submission the system generates a ticket number, and the enterprise staff will follow up.

## Viewing Details and Progress

![Ticket details](/img/manual/ticket/detail.png)

Open a ticket to view its details: status, priority, category, handler, and communication records.

![Ticket progress timeline](/img/manual/ticket/detail-timeline.png)

The "Progress" tab shows the handling timeline: creation, assignment, processing, resolution, and verification, so you can follow the whole process.

## Communicating with the Handler

Inside the ticket details you can continue replying and supplementing information; the handler receives your messages and replies in time.

## Confirming Resolved or Unresolved

When the ticket is marked resolved:

- If the problem is solved, click "Confirm resolved" and optionally rate the service;
- If it is not solved, click "Unresolved", describe the reason, and the ticket will continue to be handled.
