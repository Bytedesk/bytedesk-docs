# Bot Reception

The bot answers common questions around the clock, helps you quickly locate answers, and transfers to a human agent when needed.

## Asking a Question

Type your question in the input box and send it. The bot replies based on the enterprise knowledge base and configured model.

## Quick Questions and Recommendations

![Bot answering a FAQ](/img/manual/chat/ai/robot.png)

The bot may show popular questions or "You may want to ask" before or after replying:

- Click a recommended question to ask it directly;
- The bot's reply may carry a source article or related links for reference.

## Rating Bot Answers

Below a bot reply you may see "Helpful" and "Not helpful" buttons:

- Click "Helpful" if the answer solves your problem;
- Click "Not helpful" to give feedback, then continue asking or transfer to an agent.

Your feedback helps the enterprise improve its knowledge base.

## Transferring to an Agent

When the bot cannot handle account changes, orders, after-sales, or other complex business, click "Transfer to Agent":

1. The page enters the queue and shows the queue status;
2. After a human agent connects, continue the conversation;
3. If it is outside service hours, follow the prompt to leave a message.

## Usage Notes

- Describe your problem as completely as possible for more accurate answers;
- For questions involving personal privacy, prefer a human agent;
- Bot replies are generated automatically; verify important information with a human agent.
