# Role Management

![Role and permission page](/img/manual/admin/team/role.png)

Roles control the permission scope of members.

## Page Layout

The left side lists roles; the right side shows the permission configuration of the selected role.

## Creating a Role

1. Click "New role"
2. Enter the role name
3. Configure the permission items
4. Save

## Configuring Permissions

1. Select a role on the left
2. Check or uncheck permission items on the right
3. After saving, permissions of members under the role take effect immediately

:::tip Tip
Define roles by position (administrator, supervisor, QA specialist, etc.) for unified authorization and auditing.
:::
