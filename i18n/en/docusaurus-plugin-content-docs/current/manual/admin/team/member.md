# Member Management

![Member management page](/img/manual/admin/team/member-new.png)

Member management maintains members in the organization along with their departments, roles, and status.

## Page Layout

The left side is the department tree and the right side the member list. Members can be filtered by department.

## Member Fields

The list shows the following fields:

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Nickname / Uid / username | Member identity |
| Mobile / email / phone | Contact details |
| Department / organization | Organization membership |
| Role | Member role |
| Disabled | Whether force-signed-out |
| Login platforms | Platforms the member may sign in to |
| Created / updated | Timestamps |

## Adding a Member

1. Click "Add member"
2. Fill in the account, contact details, department, and role
3. Set the login platforms
4. Save

## Managing Members

- **Edit**: modify member information
- **Assign role**: adjust the member's roles
- **Disable / enable**: disabled members are signed out and cannot log in
- **Change department**: move the member to another department

:::note Note
A member's "Login platforms" determines the subsystems they can access, such as the admin console, agent desktop, and visitor chat.
:::
