# Dashboard

![Dashboard](/img/manual/admin/welcome-new.png)

The dashboard is the default page after login, guiding organization initialization and account security.

## Organization Selection and Creation

- **Create organization**: create one on first use and fill in the organization information
- **Switch organization**: switch the current organization when you joined several

## Account Verification

Verify your email and phone number to improve account security:

1. Click "Account verification" on the dashboard
2. Verify email: enter the code sent to your mailbox
3. Verify phone: complete the image captcha and enter the SMS code

## WeChat Official Account Subscription

Scan the QR code to subscribe to the WeChat official account and receive system notifications.

## Statistics

Enterprise and Platform editions show key statistics on the dashboard; the community edition shows an empty-state hint.
