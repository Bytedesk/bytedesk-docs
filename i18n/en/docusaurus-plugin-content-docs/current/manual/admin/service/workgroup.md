# Workgroup Management

![Workgroup management page](/img/manual/admin/service/workgroup.png)

Workgroups organize agents into reception teams and configure routing rules.

## Workgroups

Create and maintain workgroups under the "Workgroups" tab.

## Workgroup Configuration

Under "Workgroup settings" configure reception behavior. The sub-items are similar to agent settings, plus:

- **Bot / AI agent**: workgroup bot
- **Transfer to agent**: bot-to-human transfer rules
- **Triggers**: automatic trigger rules

## Workgroup Routing

Under "Routing" configure the rules that assign visitors to workgroups.

## Manual-Route Pool

Under "Manual-route pool" view route-pool conversations waiting for manual acceptance.
