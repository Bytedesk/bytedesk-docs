# Channel Management

![Channel management page](/img/manual/admin/service/channel.png)

Channels connect various visitor sources.

## Page Layout

The left side lists channels; the right side shows the configuration of the selected channel.

## Supported Channels

The system supports many access channels, including Web, App, WeChat, WeCom, Feishu, Douyin, Xiaohongshu, Weibo, JD, Pinduoduo, Messenger, WhatsApp, Telegram, Line, Email, Shopify, Lazada, and more.

## Configuring a Channel

1. Select a channel on the left
2. Fill in the channel parameters
3. Save and enable
