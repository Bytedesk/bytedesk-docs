# Help Center

![Help center page](/img/manual/admin/kb/article.png)

The help center maintains help documents for visitors.

## Page Layout

The left side lists knowledge bases; the right side shows article data.

## Article List

Create, edit, and publish help articles under the "Articles" tab.

## Translation

Manage multilingual translations of articles under the "Translation" tab.
