# Vision Capabilities

Bytedesk vision capabilities focus on helping customer service teams understand visual content such as screenshots, receipts, attachments, and other image-based materials shared by customers.

This page serves as the overview entry for vision-related capabilities. At the current stage, the primary vision capability already available in Bytedesk is OCR.

## What Vision Capabilities Mean In Bytedesk

In customer service workflows, visual content often carries key business information. Customers may send:

- error screenshots
- order pages
- shipping pages
- payment proof
- account information images

Vision capabilities are designed to turn these image-based inputs into structured information that agents and AI workflows can understand and reuse.

## Currently Available Capability

The main vision capability currently available is:

- [OCR Capabilities](./model_ocr)

OCR allows Bytedesk to extract text from screenshots and image messages, making image content searchable, copyable, and ready for downstream automation.

## How Vision Works With Other Capabilities

Vision is most valuable when combined with speech and text workflows:

- OCR turns images into text
- ASR turns speech into text
- TTS turns text into audio

Together, these capabilities create a stronger multimodal service foundation for customer support teams.

## Summary

Bytedesk vision capabilities are aimed at helping support teams process screenshots and image-based evidence more efficiently. Today, OCR is the primary production-ready vision capability, and it serves as the foundation for richer image understanding workflows in the future.
