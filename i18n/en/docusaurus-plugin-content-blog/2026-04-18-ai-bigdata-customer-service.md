## Core Applications: AI & Big Data Redefining the Service Foundation

The transformation AI and Big Data bring to customer service is essentially a shift from "labor-intensive" to "technology-intensive" operations, driven by **data-driven decision-making + intelligent automation**, covering every critical node across the service lifecycle.

### 1. Big Data: The "Insight Engine" for Customer Service

The core value of Big Data lies in breaking down data silos and enabling precise anticipation of user needs throughout their lifecycle. Weiyu integrates data across multiple channels — phone, web, app, and social — to build a three-dimensional customer profile:

- **Basic Attributes**: Age, region, purchasing power
- **Behavioral Preferences**: Browsing history, consultation records, purchase frequency
- **Demand Characteristics**: High-frequency questions, latent needs, emotional tendencies

This panoramic insight transforms customer service from "passively waiting for inquiries" to "proactively anticipating needs." When a user repeatedly browses a product's after-sales policy, the system can proactively push operation guides; when a high-value customer's service frequency drops, the system automatically triggers a dedicated follow-up — enabling churn prediction and prevention.

Real-time data analytics also optimizes resource allocation. By statistically modeling historical call volumes and consultation trends, Weiyu can precisely forecast peak-period demand (e.g., during e-commerce promotions or policy changes) and dynamically adjust the ratio of AI bots to human agents, eliminating wait times.

### 2. AI: The "Intelligent Execution Hub" for Customer Service

If Big Data is the "eyes," AI is the "brain and hands" of customer service. Weiyu's AI capabilities penetrate the entire service lifecycle:

#### Intelligent Response Layer

AI customer service powered by Large Language Models (LLM) delivers 7×24-hour instant responses, supporting multimodal interactions (voice, text, image), solving the pain point of "asking the same simple question repeatedly." Context-aware multi-turn dialogue eliminates mechanical repetitive questioning.

#### Human-AI Collaboration Layer

When issues exceed AI's scope, the system automatically generates conversation summaries and pre-fills ticket fields, enabling human agents to "take over and act immediately." The intelligent assistant module pushes real-time script recommendations and compliance reminders during live sessions, helping new agents ramp up quickly and significantly reducing training periods.

#### Intelligent Operations Layer

Weiyu leverages knowledge graph technology for authoritative knowledge updates and dynamic learning. When a company updates its after-sales policy, administrators simply upload the new document — AI automatically parses the changes and synchronizes response strategies. Unresolved issues are continuously distilled into new knowledge points, forming a self-reinforcing "serve–optimize" cycle.

## Core Value: Three Breakthroughs

Weiyu's integration of AI and Big Data delivers three core value breakthroughs:

### Efficiency Revolution

AI bots handle 70%+ of high-frequency, simple inquiries, freeing human agents to focus on complex issues and emotional support. In practice, first-contact resolution rates increase by up to 50%, and customer service operating costs drop significantly.

### Experience Upgrade

Omnichannel seamless connection, personalized responses, and instant replies free users from the frustrations of "waiting in queues" and "repeating themselves," driving substantial improvements in customer satisfaction scores.

### Value Reconstruction

Customer service is no longer a pure after-sales function — it becomes a critical touchpoint for demand discovery, business conversion, and product optimization. User conversation data feeds back into front-end product design and marketing decisions, truly transforming the "cost center" into a "growth engine."
