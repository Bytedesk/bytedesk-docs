# Speech & Vision Capabilities

Bytedesk provides a growing set of speech and vision capabilities for customer service scenarios. These capabilities help support teams move beyond plain text by understanding screenshots, converting speech into text, and turning text into spoken audio.

This page is a quick overview of the multimodal capabilities currently available in Bytedesk. For detailed descriptions of each capability, see the dedicated pages linked below.

## What This Capability Group Covers

The current speech and vision capability set includes:

- OCR for extracting text from screenshots, chat images, receipts, and other visual content
- ASR for converting voice messages and speech input into searchable text
- TTS for turning text into playable speech output

Together, these features help teams process image-based evidence, voice-based communication, and spoken service experiences in a more structured and efficient way.

## Why It Matters For Customer Service

In modern support workflows, customers do not only communicate with text. They send screenshots, voice messages, payment proofs, shipping pages, and other multimedia content. Agents also need faster ways to review, reply, and reuse information.

Bytedesk speech and vision capabilities help teams:

- Reduce manual typing and repeated listening
- Turn screenshots and voice messages into structured text
- Improve agent productivity in high-volume service scenarios
- Build a better foundation for AI summaries, bots, workflows, and quality analysis

## Recommended Reading Order

If you are evaluating these capabilities for deployment, a practical reading order is:

1. Start with OCR if your team handles many screenshots or image attachments.
2. Review ASR if your team receives voice messages or wants speech-to-text input.
3. Review TTS if you plan to add voice playback, spoken responses, or AI voice output.

## Summary

Bytedesk already provides a practical speech and vision foundation for customer service, centered around OCR, ASR, and TTS. These capabilities can be used independently or combined to support richer agent workflows and future AI-driven service experiences.
