# Digital Human Service

Bytedesk Digital Human Service provides AI-powered, avatar-based customer support across web, mobile, and call center channels.

## Key capabilities

- Real-time digital human conversation for text and voice scenarios.
- Unified knowledge grounding with enterprise FAQ and business documents.
- Multi-channel access for website, app, hotline, and embedded service entry points.
- Human handoff when confidence is low or users request a live agent.
- Service analytics for conversation quality, escalation rate, and intent coverage.

## Typical scenarios

- 24x7 reception for pre-sales consultation.
- Standardized first-line support for repetitive customer questions.
- Campaign and event consultation with consistent service tone.
- Voice-based service assistant for hotline and IVR workflows.

## Value for teams

- Reduce repetitive workload for agents.
- Improve response consistency and service availability.
- Shorten onboarding time with reusable service playbooks.
- Build a scalable customer service operation with AI and human collaboration.
